This is the official website for FIKA, a premium multi-cuisine cafe located in Civil Lines, Prayagraj (Allahabad). Rooted in the Swedish tradition of fika — the ritual of pausing to connect — the site reflects the cafe's warm, design-led dine-in experience: fine coffee, curated global dining, and effortless conversation.
About FIKA
FIKA is Civil Lines' aesthetic haven for fine coffee, curated global dining, and effortless conversation. Surrounded by warm tones and lush foliage, it's built for slowing down, sharing multi-cuisine plates, and savouring the moment.
Cuisine: Pan-Asian & Japanese, Italian, specialty snacks, cocktails, specialty coffee, desserts
Services: Dine-in, takeaway, home delivery
